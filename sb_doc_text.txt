w:rsidP="79C011C1" w14:paraId="13C9553F" wp14:textId="77777777" xmlns:wp14="http://schemas.microsoft.com/office/word/2010/wordml">Shipping Bill (SB) is a mandatory document issued by shipping lines which serves as a declaration to customs authorities, providing details about the goods being shipped, their value, quantity, and destination. This document is essential for customs clearance, facilitating the movement of goods out of the country, and plays a vital role in claiming duty drawbacks and export incentives. Traditionally handled via email or manual systems, Shipping Bill is now being digitized through API integration to improve turnaround time, reduce manual errors, and ensure regulatory compliance.
w:rsidP="79C011C1" w14:paraId="213B7BAE" wp14:textId="77777777" xmlns:wp14="http://schemas.microsoft.com/office/word/2010/wordml">The Shipping Bill Integration enables seamless, real-time submission of requests directly from the Payor's internal system to the ODeX System. This not only simplifies submission of Shipping Bill requests but also enhances operational efficiency, especially in high-volume export scenarios.
